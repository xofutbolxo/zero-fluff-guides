Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

THE SHORT VERSION

Zero Fluff Guides (zerofluffguides.com) is published by Comwares Inc. This site does not collect your personal data. There are no forms, no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking code.

Book worksheets. The free worksheets at zerofluffguides.com/tools save your answers in your own browser's storage on your device, so they are still there when you come back. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time with the "Clear all my answers" button, which sits with the other buttons at the top of each worksheet, or by clearing your browser's site data.

SERVICES THE SITE USES

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Fonts. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. We don't receive or use that information.

Amazon. Book links take you to Amazon. Once you're there, Amazon's privacy policy applies.

IF YOU EMAIL US

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We don't add you to a mailing list, and we don't sell or share your details.
